# QR Box Reader

**Scan QR codes straight from your screen, no phone required.**

Draw a box around any QR code on a web page, and QR Box Reader shows you the link hidden inside it.

## The Problem

QR codes are made to be scanned with a phone camera. That works until it doesn't:

- A coworker sends a QR code as an image in a chat, and your phone is dead, lost, or in another room.
- You're working on a laptop and the code is on the same screen you're looking at, so there's nothing to point a camera at.
- You only have one device, and it's the one displaying the code.

Usually the only options are to find a charger, borrow someone's phone, or upload the image to a random third-party website and hope it's trustworthy.

## The Solution

QR Box Reader lets you scan a QR code on the very screen where it's displayed.

1. Open the image containing the QR code.
2. Click the extension icon.
3. Drag a box around the QR code.
4. See the decoded link right away.

You can then copy the link, or open it after you've checked it.

## Features

- **Point-and-select scanning:** draw a box around only the QR code you want, even on a busy page.
- **No second device:** everything happens on the computer you're already using.
- **Safe by default:** links are shown to you first and never opened automatically. The website's domain is displayed separately from the full link, so suspicious-looking addresses are easy to spot.
- **Quick actions:** copy the link with one click, or open it when you're ready.
- **Works on any page:** chat apps, emails, documents, or anywhere a QR code appears on screen.

## Who Is It For?

Anyone who has received a QR code on their computer and just wants the link, including remote workers, people without a spare device, and anyone who would rather not hunt for their phone.

## A Note on Safety

QR codes can lead to phishing and scam websites, just like any other link. Always check the domain before opening a link, especially if the QR code came from someone you don't know or was unexpected.
